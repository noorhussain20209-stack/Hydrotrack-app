Privacy Policy for HydroTrack

Last updated: August 2026

HydroTrack does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All information you enter (water intake, reminder settings) is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted anywhere.

HydroTrack does not use analytics, ads, or third-party tracking of any kind.

If you have questions about this policy, you may contact the developer through the app store listing page.
